# What we need from Bhoomi

Her portfolio is laid out like a social profile: a grid of posts, story highlights, short reels and a Messages inbox. The code is in place. What makes it look premium is **real photos, screenshots and short screen recordings**. Until those arrive, the site shows plain placeholders. Nothing goes live without her OK.

Everything below can be sent in any order. Items 1–4 matter most.

## A message you can forward

> Hi Bhoomi! Your portfolio site is being built, laid out like an Instagram profile, with your projects as posts and your experience as story highlights. To make it look great, could you send:
>
> 1. Your GitHub username
> 2. 2–3 good photos of you (one clear headshot, plus a couple of you working or at an event)
> 3. Screenshots of SmartBlog AI, the Conversational AI Web App and the AI Resume Shortlisting Tool (desktop and phone if you have them), and the GitHub or live links if they're public
> 4. A 15–30 second screen recording of each project in use (no voice needed)
> 5. Where messages from the site should reach you: email, Telegram, or both
> 6. A copy of your résumé PDF without your phone number (or say it's fine to publish the current one)
>
> Optional: CyberCup photos or certificate, certificate images with verification links, a domain name you like, and 2–3 short quotes from people you've worked with (with their OK to show them).
>
> The full list with sizes is below if you want it. Thanks!
